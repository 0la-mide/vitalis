# Storylines

**Built: A. One night, one ID.** Recommended because a stranger follows one woman from "something is wrong" to "a
hospital is ready for you", and every feature appears as a step in her night, not as a list.

## A. One night, one ID (built)
Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: object-to-object chain (each tap causes the next screen, the hospital's
click causes her phone to change). 2:07 AM, 34 weeks pregnant, bleeding. The old way (three hospitals that cannot
help) is struck through; VITALIS: three taps, the browser asks for her location, an emergency plan with only hospitals
that can treat her; the hospital accepts on its screen and her phone says go now; the chemist, reception with her name
and NHIA number, the doctor's AI catching an allergy from another hospital, lab and radiology, her consent; claim and
mark. Look: the app's own paper, brand green and calm teal, IBM Plex Sans, real screens at 2x. Sound: soft taps,
whooshes on world changes, one notification, one impact on the mark; music bed to add (see REVIEW.md).

## B. The ID card (not built)
Device: one object across apps. The VITALIS ID itself is the camera's subject: it is minted on a phone, slides into
the hospital's Incoming, the doctor's record, the lab label and the patient's QR. Form: endless zoom into the ID's
characters, each opening the next screen.

## C. Three phones, one hospital (not built)
Device: split screen to one. A family, a chemist and a hospital work at once in three panes that collapse into the
hospital's screen at the moment it accepts. Form: split screen to one, cut on the beat.
