# VITALIS · Teleprompter script

Play **VITALIS-pitch-presenter.mp4** (music and effects, no narration) behind you.
Timecodes are when the picture changes. Speak to the room, not the screen.

`/` = short breath · `//` = full stop, let it land · **bold** = lean on it · *(cue)* = what's on screen

---

### Delivery notes
- Pace: slower than feels natural. The silences are the drama.
- Stand still on the bold words; move only between sections.
- At 0:36 ("Now watch this"), turn slightly toward the screen, then back to the room on "Blocked."
- If the video runs ahead of you, skip a line; never rush to catch up. Land the last line on the end card.
- Total spoken: about 3 minutes with the intro and close; the film itself is 62 seconds.
