# VITALIS · Teleprompter script

Timecodes are when the picture changes. You are the only sound in the room. Speak to the room, not the screen.

`/` = short breath · `//` = full stop, let it land · **bold** = lean on it · *(cue)* = what's on screen

### Delivery notes
- The film has no sound. Your voice is the soundtrack, so let the silences be silent; don't fill them.
- Pace: slower than feels natural. Each section fits its scene at an easy pace; if you finish early, wait for the picture.
- Stand still on the bold words; move only between sections.
- At 0:36 ("Now. This is Nigeria."), step toward the room. This is the moment that answers "but what about network?"
- If the video runs ahead of you, skip a line; never rush to catch up. Land the last line on the end card.
- Total: about 2 minutes 30 seconds with the intro and close; the film itself is 1:46.
